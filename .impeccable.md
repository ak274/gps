## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are parents and families evaluating schools, along with visitors looking for clear school information. They need fast trust signals, a strong sense of academic quality, and an easy path to admissions, contact, and programs.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel confident, premium, and inspiring. The emotional goal is to give families a sense of assurance, ambition, and warmth without slipping into something flashy or excessive.

### Aesthetic Direction
The visual direction is bold academic luxury with a mixed light-and-dark presentation. Dark sections should feel ceremonial and high-trust, while light sections should feel open, polished, and readable. The site should feel modern and elevated, but never over-designed, childish, or noisy.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with trust: important details should feel official, composed, and easy to verify.
2. Use contrast with restraint: dramatic sections are welcome, but every bold move must serve clarity.
3. Make academics feel aspirational: present the school as disciplined, future-facing, and premium.
4. Keep parent journeys obvious: admissions, contact, and school information must stay easy to scan.
5. Avoid visual excess: no clutter, no gimmicks, and no loud effects that reduce credibility.
